Xaver Varnus

Tour Manager & Personal Assistant · 2012-2014

Xaver Varnus is one of the world's most celebrated organists and a cultural provocateur. He's performed in the greatest concert halls across five continents-from Notre-Dame to Carnegie Hall-bringing classical organ music to audiences who thought they'd never care about it. Known for his theatrical flair, virtuosity, and ability to make centuries-old music feel urgent and alive. Working with someone operating at that level of excellence and courage changed everything for me.

Xaver didn't just hire me-he mentored me. As his tour manager and assistant, I organized sold-out concerts, handled logistics, bookings, social media. But more than that, he showed me the professional world of arts and performing artists from the inside. How discipline, passion, and relentless excellence look in practice.

He railed me onto a journey I'm still on-connecting culture with people, building experiences that matter.
